Features

Everything the trainer can do

Six core toggles built for builders, breeders and sandbox tinkerers. Each one edits live memory, so changes apply the moment you flip the switch.

💰

Infinite Cash

Lock your zoo budget at a fixed high value. Build the elephant house, the aquarium wing and the monorail without ever checking the balance sheet again.

🔬

Instant Animal Research

Complete every research tree for every species instantly. Enrichment, diet and breeding knowledge unlock immediately so new habitats are fully optimized from day one.

🧱

Free-Build Mode Toggle

Ignore placement costs and object restrictions. Flip a single switch to bypass build limits, then flip it back when you want the normal economy to return.

⏩

Speed Hack

Multiply game speed beyond the vanilla slider to fast-forward breeding programs and guest throughput — or slow the simulation down to a crawl for precision builds.

🌿

No Habitat Requirements

Disable terrain, plant and space requirements for any habitat. Animals stay content even in experimental enclosures while you prototype wild new layouts.

🧠

Live Memory Editor

Reads and writes the running game process directly. No save-file patching, no modified executables — toggles take effect instantly and revert cleanly.

How it works

Three steps to a limitless zoo

Setup takes under two minutes on a standard Windows 10 or 11 install.

1

Download and launch the trainer

Grab the latest build from the download page and run it as administrator. Windows SmartScreen may ask for confirmation — the tool is free and open source, so you can inspect the source at any time.

2

Start Planet Zoo 2 and attach

Launch the game in single-player or sandbox mode, then hit “Attach” in the trainer. It scans the live process and lists the editable values — cash, research progress, speed multiplier and habitat flags.

3

Toggle and build
